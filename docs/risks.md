# Risks, Blockers and Watch List

> **This is the single place to look before shipping anything public.**
>
> Every unconfirmed fact and every open decision from any other document rolls up here.
> If a doc says "UNCONFIRMED" or "PROVISIONAL", there is a row for it below.
>
> **Owner** names who can actually resolve it — several of these cannot be resolved from
> the desk and need a third party. Those have lead times measured in weeks, which is why
> this document was written before the product specification.

Status values: `open` · `chasing` · `resolved` · `accepted` (knowingly living with it)
Last reviewed: 4 September, 2026
